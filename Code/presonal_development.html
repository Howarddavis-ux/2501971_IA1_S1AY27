The Richest Man in Babylon

The biggest take away from this book is that a person's success isn’t always tied to their profession but the mentality they bring towards managing their “stream of gold”. If Arkhad, who was only a scribe managed to achieve great riches, just means that I also can. Even though I don’t have a stream of gold doesn’t mean I can’t divert a portion of funds that I manage to come across to saving and also adjust my expenditure to secure the coins in my purse. I have never been tied to material things but I too have spent money on unnecessary things, creating a to budget to cover essentials while placing a portion aside for myself because what I earn is my to keep.
