AI as a Tool

AI acts as a tool that can quickly provide solutions to difficult problems and summary information without having to spend minutes to hours manually scouring the internet, textbooks, or other sources. Leaving additional time for students to better understand the principle behind the solution and apply their knowledge in practice. In addition, the additional time students will have to pursue personal interests, which is beneficial as it decreases their stress and prevents burnout(College of Business, 2023). There are numerous examples which prove that AI can be used by students to accomplish some achievements in a very short period of time. One example, according to reports, MIT students have managed to create a jet engine in only four with the assistance of AI (Ornitz, 2026). It proves that AI is an effective way to increase efficiency when doing something complicated by both students and developers.
